Pakistan Cities and Flight Routes

Hover over a city to highlight its connected flight routes.



Legend: Four Visual Encodings

1. Position: Each city is positioned using longitude and latitude.

2. Circle size: Small population   Medium population   Large population

3. Circle color: Hub (4 or more routes)   Non-hub (fewer than 4 routes)

4. Line width: Fewer flights   Medium flights   More flights per week

Hover interaction: Connected routes turn orange; unrelated routes become faint.
